The Craft

No machines rushing. Just hands, leather & patience.

Every pair at Classic Shoes begins as a single hide of pure leather, chosen by hand at the workshop in Cantt Bazar. It is cut, skived, stitched and finished by craftsmen who have spent years at the bench — the way Peshawari chappals and formal shoes have always been made in this region.

01
Hand-picked leather

Full-grain hides checked for grain, thickness and natural shine before a single cut.

02
Cut & stitched by hand

Uppers are hand-cut and saddle-stitched, so seams stay strong for years of daily wear.

03
Finished & polished

Edges are burnished, soles pressed and each pair polished and inspected before packing.
